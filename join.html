At the Palma Lab, we believe that doing great science and enjoying the process should go hand in hand. We are committed to creating a supportive and collaborative environment where every lab member can grow, explore ambitious ideas, and develop into an independent scientist.

Graduate Students & Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome prospective graduate students and postdoctoral researchers interested in combining quantitative and experimental approaches to investigate gene regulatory networks.

Applicants with backgrounds in molecular biology, microbiology, mathematics, computer science, biomedical engineering, or related fields are encouraged to get in touch.

Undergraduate & Rotation Students

We welcome rotation and undergraduate researchers year-round.

How to Apply

Email Cristina at csantosd[at]uwyo.edu with your CV and a brief cover letter sharing your research interests and how they fit with our lab's vision.
