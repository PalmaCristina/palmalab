Join Us

The Palma Lab is recruiting students and researchers interested in understanding microbial systems through a combination of experimental and computational approaches.

We welcome applicants from diverse scientific backgrounds, including molecular biology, microbiology, bioengineering, computer science, mathematics, and related disciplines.

Graduate Students

We welcome prospective PhD and MSc students interested in quantitative and systems approaches to microbial biology.

Applicants should have a background in molecular biology, microbiology, bioengineering, computer science, mathematics, or a related field. Previous experience in both experimental and computational research is not required.

Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome inquiries from prospective postdoctoral researchers interested in microbial systems biology, gene regulation, mathematical modeling, synthetic biology, or related areas.

Undergraduate Students

Undergraduate students interested in gaining research experience in experimental or computational biology are encouraged to get in touch.

How to Apply

If you are interested in joining the Palma Lab, please email Cristina Palma with:

  • Your CV or résumé
  • A brief description of your research interests
  • Your academic background and relevant research experience
  • Why you are interested in joining the Palma Lab

Please send inquiries to csantosd@uwyo.edu.
