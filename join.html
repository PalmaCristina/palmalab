Palma Lab · Opportunities

Join Us

We are building an interdisciplinary team to study microbial stress adaptation using computational, quantitative, and experimental approaches.

Graduate Students

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students interested in combining quantitative and experimental approaches to understand microbial regulatory systems.

Applicants with backgrounds in molecular biology, microbiology, mathematics, computer science, biomedical engineering, quantitative biology, or related fields are encouraged to get in touch.

Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome inquiries from researchers interested in computational biology, systems biology, microbial physiology, gene regulation, mathematical modeling, or synthetic biology.

Candidates interested in developing interdisciplinary projects spanning theoretical and experimental approaches are particularly encouraged to contact us.

Undergraduate Students

Undergraduate students interested in research at the intersection of biology, computation, and quantitative modeling are welcome to inquire about opportunities in the lab.

Previous research experience is not required. Curiosity, commitment, and an interest in learning across disciplines are especially valued.

How to Apply

Please email Cristina with a brief description of your research interests and why you are interested in joining the Palma Lab.

For graduate student and postdoctoral inquiries, please include your CV.
