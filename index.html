Quantitative systems biology of microbial stress response and adaptation

We study how microorganisms sense environmental changes and reorganize gene expression to survive stress. By combining mathematical models of gene regulatory networks with quantitative experiments and synthetic biology, we aim to uncover the regulatory mechanisms that control microbial adaptation and develop predictive models of cellular behavior.
