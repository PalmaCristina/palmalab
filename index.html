Quantitative Systems Biology of Microbial Stress Adaptation

We investigate how microorganisms sense, respond to, and adapt to environmental stress. By combining mathematical modeling, systems biology, synthetic biology, and quantitative experiments, we uncover the regulatory principles that shape microbial behavior.

From regulatory mechanisms to predictive models

Biological responses emerge from interactions between genes, proteins, cells, and their environments. Our goal is to understand how these interactions generate adaptive behavior and to develop quantitative models capable of predicting how microbial systems respond to new conditions.

Integrating mathematical models with quantitative experiments to understand and predict microbial behavior.
