Quantitative Systems Biology of Microbial Stress Adaptation

We investigate how microorganisms sense, respond to, and adapt to stress. By combining mathematical modeling, systems biology, and synthetic biology, our goal is to uncover the gene network desgin rules that govern microbial behavior.

Research

Microbial Stress Response

Understanding how microorganisms detect environmental perturbations and coordinate adaptive responses.

Gene Regulatory Dynamics

Investigating how regulatory networks generate dynamic cellular responses across time and environmental conditions.

Predictive Systems Biology

Integrating mathematical models with quantitative experiments to explain and predict microbial behavior.
