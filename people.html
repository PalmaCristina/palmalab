Cristina Palma, PhD

Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor

Cristina is a computational biologist interested in the regulatory mechanisms that allow microorganisms to sense, respond to, and adapt to environmental stress.

Her research combines mathematical models of gene regulatory networks with quantitative experiments and synthetic biology to uncover how regulatory architecture shapes microbial behavior.

Cristina received her PhD from Tampere University in Finland and completed postdoctoral research at Rice University.
