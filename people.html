Principal Investigator

Cristina Palma, PhD

Assistant Professor
Department of Molecular Biology
School of Computing
University of Wyoming

Cristina is a computational biologist interested in understanding how microorganisms sense, respond to, and adapt to environmental stress. Her research combines mathematical modeling with quantitative experimental approaches to investigate the dynamics and design principles of microbial gene regulatory networks.

She received her PhD in Biomedical Sciences and Engineering from Tampere University, Finland, and completed postdoctoral training at Rice University at the interface of computational systems biology and synthetic biology.
