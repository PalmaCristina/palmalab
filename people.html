Team

We are building an interdisciplinary group focused on understanding microbial stress adaptation through computational, quantitative, and experimental approaches.

Principal Investigator

Cristina Palma, PhD

Assistant Professor

Department of Molecular Biology
School of Computing
University of Wyoming

Cristina is a computational biologist whose research focuses on uncovering the genetic regulatory mechanisms that enable microorganisms to respond and adapt to stress conditions.

Her group combines mathematical models of gene regulatory networks with synthetic biology and quantitative experiments to perturb adaptation and uncover the principles that shape microbial behavior.

Cristina received her PhD from Tampere University in Finland and completed her postdoctoral research at Rice University.
